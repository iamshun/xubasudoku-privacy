許爸的數獨隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 26 日

本政策說明 Android 與 iOS 應用程式「許爸的數獨」如何處理遊戲資料。若有隱私相關問題，請寄信至 17apphouse@gmail.com。

我們處理的資料

許爸的數獨不需要註冊或登入。應用程式會在裝置的應用程式專用儲存空間保存數獨題目、棋盤與筆記、錯誤與提示使用次數、遊玩時間、完成紀錄、上次選擇的難度與模式，以及語言和顯示設定。這些資料用於繼續遊戲、顯示進度、提供每日挑戰與保留偏好設定。每日挑戰使用裝置的本地日期產生題目。

應用程式沒有廣告或分析服務，也沒有開發者自建的雲端帳號或遊戲資料伺服器；應用程式本身不會主動把上述遊戲資料傳送給開發者。

備份、分享與系統服務

你可以在應用程式內選擇「匯出備份」，透過裝置的系統分享功能，把包含上述遊戲進度與設定的 JSON 備份檔交給你選擇的應用程式或儲存位置。你也可以從自行選取的備份檔匯入資料。匯出後，所選服務如何處理該檔案，依其隱私權政策及你的設定而定。

此外，Android 的系統備份功能可能依裝置和帳號設定，將應用程式資料備份至 Google 或裝置供應商的備份服務，或在換機時轉移資料。iOS 的 iCloud 備份也可能依你的裝置和帳號設定，包含應用程式資料或在換機時移轉資料。這些系統功能與應用程式內的手動匯出不同；是否啟用及資料如何保護、保存與刪除，由相關系統服務和你的裝置設定決定。

保存與刪除

裝置上的遊戲資料會保留到你清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。若你曾手動匯出備份，也需到所選儲存位置自行刪除備份檔。若使用 Android 或 iOS 系統備份，請到裝置或相關帳號的備份設定管理該副本。

政策更新

若應用程式的資料處理方式有變，我們會更新本政策及上方日期。請以本頁公布的最新版本為準。

許爸的數獨 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Android and iOS app 許爸的數獨 handles game data. For privacy questions, contact 17apphouse@gmail.com.

Data handled by the app

You do not need to register or sign in. The app stores Sudoku puzzles, boards and notes, mistake and hint counts, play time, completion records, the last selected difficulty and mode, and language and display settings in its app-specific storage on your device. It uses this information to resume games, show progress, provide daily puzzles and remember your preferences. Daily puzzles are generated using your device's local date.

The app has no ads, analytics service, developer-run cloud account or game-data server. The app itself does not actively send this game data to the developer.

Backups, sharing and system services

If you choose Export backup in the app, your device's system share menu can send a JSON file containing the game progress and settings described above to the app or storage location you select. You can also import a backup file you select. After export, the selected service handles that file according to its privacy policy and your settings.

Android's system backup feature may also, depending on your device and account settings, back up app data to Google or a device manufacturer's backup service, or transfer data when you move to another device. On iOS, iCloud Backup may include app data or transfer it to a new device, depending on your device and account settings. These system features are separate from the app's manual export. The relevant system services and your device settings control whether they run and how the data is protected, retained and deleted.

Retention and deletion

Game data on your device remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. If you exported a backup manually, delete that copy from the storage location you selected. If you use Android or iOS system backup, manage its copy in your device or account backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the date above. The latest version published on this page applies.
